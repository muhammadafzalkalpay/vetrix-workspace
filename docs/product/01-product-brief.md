# Vetrix product brief

## One line

The AI front desk for veterinary clinics. Answers every call, books the right appointment in the right room with the right person, and brings pets back when they are due.

## Who buys

The owner or practice manager of an independent small-animal clinic, or a group of 2 to 10 locations, with 1 to 8 veterinarians per location. The buying trigger is one of three things: missed calls during busy hours, front-desk turnover, or an overdue wellness and vaccine list nobody has time to work.

## What it does in v1

1. **AI front desk.** Answers inbound calls around the clock. Handles new and existing clients, books, reschedules, cancels, answers policy questions, triages emergencies and transfers them.
2. **Resource-aware scheduling.** Books against exam rooms, surgery and dental suites, equipment, and provider role and skill. Enforces clinic rules: fasting before surgery, pre-op bloodwork, technician appointments only when a doctor is on site.
3. **Reminders.** Wellness, vaccine, parasite prevention, dental, senior, and post-op reminders. Household-aware, so a family with three pets gets one message, not three.

## What it does not do in v1

Medical records, clinical notes, invoicing, inventory, prescriptions, lab results, or sync with the practice management system. Those stay in the clinic's PIMS (ezyVet, Cornerstone, AVImark, Shepherd, Digitail, Vetspire). Vetrix sits in front of the PIMS and hands off through the daily schedule export and the clinic's existing habits.

## Why a clinic picks Vetrix

Weave and PetDesk are messaging layers. They remind, they do not answer the phone and book against real room and doctor constraints. The phone AI startups answer the phone but book into a flat calendar, so staff rebook half of what the AI schedules. Vetrix does both, with the owner-plus-pet household model that clinics actually need.

## Positioning line

Every call answered. Every pet brought back.

## Pricing

Placeholder. Per location per month, three tiers by monthly call volume. Set by the product owner before launch.

## Dashboard metrics

Calls answered by AI versus transferred. Bookings made by AI. Missed calls recovered. Reminders sent versus appointments booked from reminders. No-show rate. Overdue patients recovered this month.
